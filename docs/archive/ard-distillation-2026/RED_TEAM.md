- **REPORTED** — relayed from a search result, an abstract, a tool summary, or a repository document's own summary of a paper not opened here.
- **INFERRED** — reasoning from the above; the reasoning is shown.

## 1. "Your 0.092 pp is the spread of one random stream inside one parent. It is not the noise floor of anything you have compared, and it is not the noise floor of anything the field reports."

**The objection.** The thesis will present 0.092 pp as "the run-to-run noise floor after the learning-rate decay". The six runs behind it share a parent checkpoint, the epoch-100 prefix, the data order and the augmentation view; the only streams that differ are the training attack's random start and the Python, NumPy and global Torch streams (VERIFIED, `docs/plans/0092-post-decay-floor-calibration.md` lines 76–88, table "random source / replicate behaviour"; record `docs/experiments/ard_post_decay_floor_v1.json`, `design.description`). Training is bit-deterministic, so nothing else moved (VERIFIED, `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/ERT_RSLAD_REAL_DATA_TRAINING_DETERMINISM.md`: e104/e109/e114 state dicts byte-identical across two runs). What was measured is therefore the spread produced by re-seeding the inner maximisation, and only that.

The project's own measurement-design document defines the "type (a) RNG replicate" floor as varying "the random number stream after the fork (data order, augmentation draws, attack random starts)" (VERIFIED, `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/MEASUREMENT_DESIGN.md` §2.1 table). Plan 0092 varied one of those three. The number in `docs/POST_DECAY_FLOOR.md` is thus narrower than the quantity its own standard defines.

Four consequences, each independently damaging.

(a) **It is not the floor of the project's own screens.** Every historical e114 treated-versus-control contrast ran with `continuation_seed: null` on both arms, so treated and control shared every stream (VERIFIED, `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/METHOD_DIRECTIONS.md` "Verification" section; `docs/plans/0095-cohort-placebo-floor.md` "Why the existing floor does not answer this"). A zero-effect treatment under that design is bit-identical to its control; its floor is "how far a small weight perturbation carries the trajectory by e114", and it has never been measured. `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/POST_DECAY_FLOOR_RECLASSIFICATION.md` judged nine contrasts against 0.092 pp; the repository itself now says that was a floor of the wrong kind. As of today the measured floor applies to no contrast the project has run.

(b) **It is not the floor of any claim about the field.** Published gains are independent-seed, official-test, AutoAttack, end-of-training quantities. The project's own independent-seed spread at e199 on its held-out CE-PGD20 endpoint is a per-run SD of 0.339 pp for I100, so a two-run difference SD of 0.48 pp (VERIFIED, `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/MEASUREMENT_DESIGN.md` §2.5 table, line 298). That is five times the paired figure, and it sits inside the 0.1 to 0.6 pp range the literature already reports for ResNet-18-class models (VERIFIED, `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/ARD_VERSUS_AT_ASSESSMENT.md` §3.2). The project's own data confirm the field's numbers; they do not indict them. The floor document itself says "Nothing here transfers to a different horizon, a different endpoint, or the official test split" (VERIFIED, `docs/POST_DECAY_FLOOR.md`, last section). So the sentence "this project's floor is 0.092 pp; against it single-run claims of +0.5 pp are indistinguishable from nothing" (the argument of `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/essays/PRIOR_ART_STATE_CONDITIONED_AUGMENTATION.md` §3, VERIFIED) compares a paired within-parent quantity with unpaired between-group claims. The comparison is invalid in the thesis's own terms, and the paragraph that makes it must go.

(c) **Even for the paired design, the attack-only replicate may understate the floor.** Pre-decay, every perturbation type saturated at the same 1.2 pp, so the choice of stream did not matter (VERIFIED, `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/MEASUREMENT_DESIGN.md` §2.4; `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/ERT_RSLAD_RNG_SOURCE_DECOMPOSITION.md`). Post-decay the chaos is damped and saturation cannot be assumed. At e104 the independent-seed per-run SD is 0.226 pp against a paired within-parent SD of 0.113 pp (VERIFIED, 0.226 from `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/MEASUREMENT_DESIGN.md` §2.5 line 310; within-parent from the record's `pooled.sigma_within_pp` at e104 converted from the two-run figure 0.159). INFERRED: a replicate that also re-seeds the data order lands somewhere between those two numbers, and the thesis does not know where.

(d) **The floor is the wrong variance component for any claim about a method.** The block MDE arithmetic `2.8 σ_d / √k` treats each parent-level contrast as an independent draw whose only noise is the replicate floor. That holds only if the treatment effect does not vary by parent. The repository shows it does: on dev-1 all nine e114 arms are positive and seven exceed the highest of three dev-1 control replicates; on dev-2, five of nine go to zero or below once re-baselined (VERIFIED, `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/METHOD_DIRECTIONS.md` §1.1). That is a parent-by-treatment interaction larger than 0.092 pp. The noise a sentence like "PM improves robustness" needs is the between-parent SD of the effect, and with two parents that quantity has one degree of freedom.

The point estimate is also quoted as if it were the measurement. The 95 % interval on σ_d at e114 is 0.055 to 0.265 pp on four degrees of freedom (VERIFIED, record `pooled.sigma_d_ci95_pp`). The upper end is the lower end of the bracket the number is said to replace. "A four-fold correction" is a 1.5-fold to 7-fold correction.

**Grade.** (a) ANSWERABLE WITH plan 0095 as written: three class-matched random-cohort placebo arms plus one fresh control per parent, 2.9 GPU-h (VERIFIED, plan 0095). (c) ANSWERABLE WITH six further forks from the same two parents, e100 to e114, re-seeding the data-order stream as well as the attack stream: 2.2 GPU-h plus a small change to `continuation_seed`. (d) ANSWERABLE WITH plan 0093's six new parents (about 16 GPU-h for parents, 31 GPU-h for forks; VERIFIED, plan 0093 §4 and `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/METHOD_DIRECTIONS.md` §2). (b) is not an experiment. It is a mislabelling, fixed only in the writing: name the number "the within-parent, attack-stream replicate floor at e114 on validation CE-PGD20" with all four labels every time it appears (exactly the rule `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/NUMERIC_CONSISTENCY_AUDIT.md` pattern 2 already demands), and delete every sentence that sets it beside a published gain.

## 2. "Your only official-test result was 'confirmed' by a rule that passes one time in eight under the null, and the effect halved on the way from your screen to the endpoint the field reads."

**The objection.** The preregistered rule for plan 0091 was: confirmed if the I100-minus-control AutoAttack difference is positive in all three seeds (VERIFIED, `docs/plans/0091-i100-official-test-autoattack.md` "Preregistered decision rule"). Under a null of no effect that rule passes with probability 1/8 = 0.125. The result was +0.41 / +0.06 / +0.46 pp, mean +0.31, SD 0.218, paired t(2) = 2.46 (VERIFIED, `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/ERT_RSLAD_I100_OFFICIAL_TEST_AUTOATTACK.md`; t and p = 0.125 stated in plan 0091's own completion section, lines 236–250). Two-sided p for t(2) = 2.46 is about 0.13. One of the three seeds is six test images.

The project applied a stricter standard to everyone else. `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/EVIDENCE_RECLASSIFICATION.md` row E1 dismisses "both seeds positive" because its null probability is 0.25 (VERIFIED). A sign test on three seeds at 0.125 is the same kind of rule, half as loose, and it is the rule behind the one CONFIRMED label in the project. The examiner will read the two documents side by side.

The screen-to-endpoint attenuation compounds it. The same six runs gave +0.78 / +0.68 / +0.62 pp on validation CE-PGD20 and +0.41 / +0.06 / +0.46 pp on official-test AutoAttack (VERIFIED, same report). The screening endpoint overstates the reported endpoint by a factor of about two, and the spread of the difference triples (SD 0.08 pp on validation, 0.218 pp on AutoAttack; VERIFIED, `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/NUMERIC_CONSISTENCY_AUDIT.md` findings #2 and #3). The project has no floor of any kind for the endpoint the field reads. INFERRED: if the official-test paired floor is near 0.2 pp, as those three differences suggest, then the validation floor understates it by a factor of two, and every power calculation in plans 0093 and 0095 that is sized on 0.092 pp is sized for the wrong endpoint.

What is stronger than the thesis currently claims: the best-checkpoint AutoAttack difference is +0.30 / +0.45 / +0.42 pp, SD 0.079, t(2) = 8.5 (VERIFIED, same report and plan 0091 line 253). "Best" is selected on the validation split and evaluated on the official test set, which is the standard legitimate protocol; the selection effect the plan worries about applies equally to both arms of a paired difference. The thesis undersells its best evidence and oversells its preregistered one.

**Grade.** ANSWERABLE WITH two more confirmation seeds on I100 versus `CROP_SUFFIX` to e199 with AutoAttack on best and last (four runs, about 20 GPU-h plus 8 evaluations), which takes the paired t to four degrees of freedom; and with the AutoAttack evaluation of plan 0093's twelve e199 control forks (about 12 GPU-h), which gives the official-test paired floor. ANSWERABLE TODAY in the writing: report the last-checkpoint result as "direction replicated in three of three seeds; magnitude +0.31 ± 0.22 pp, not significant at five seeds' worth of evidence", report the best-checkpoint result beside it as the sharper estimate with its selection caveat, and never write CONFIRMED without the rule's null rate next to it.

## 3. "The field already reports the noise you say it lacks, the design you call new has a name, and the one paper you now hold up as the frontier reports seven-run standard deviations for every baseline."

**The objection.** The intended claim is that the field could not judge its gains because it lacked a floor (VERIFIED as intent, `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/ARD_VERSUS_AT_ASSESSMENT.md` §4 rank 1: "the field could not have, because it lacks the floor"). That is false for adversarial training and only nine-tenths true for distillation.

For AT: Rice et al. 2020, Gowal et al. 2020 (10 seeds), Pang et al. 2021 (5), Rebuffi et al. 2021 (10), Tack et al. 2022 (5), IDBH 2023 (3, every row), VAIR 2023 (4), ADR 2024 (5), AR-AT 2024 (5) all report independent-seed spreads on the endpoint that matters (VERIFIED as a table with citations in `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/ARD_VERSUS_AT_ASSESSMENT.md` §3.2). DAT (NeurIPS 2024) reports seven-run means and SDs for TRADES + AWP, TRADES + AWP + SWA, DAJAT, IDBH and its own method on ResNet-18, and its PGD-AT baseline agrees with this repository's PGD-AT to the second decimal (VERIFIED as recorded in `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/SMALL_MODEL_AT_2024_2026.md` §0 from the arXiv PDF; the "7 runs" caption independently REPORTED by web search of the NeurIPS proceedings PDF). DAT's rerun of IDBH + AWP + SWA lands at 52.31 ± 0.26 against IDBH's own 52.46 (VERIFIED, same section): a cross-group reproduction within 0.15 pp. So on the AT side the field measures its noise and reproduces across groups. The thesis knows this — the survey is in the repository — but the criticism in `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/essays/PRIOR_ART_STATE_CONDITIONED_AUGMENTATION.md` §3 and the thesis outline are still written as if it did not.

For ARD: nine of ten method papers report one run, SAAD reports three (VERIFIED, `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/ARD_VERSUS_AT_ASSESSMENT.md` §3.1). That is a literature observation, defensible and worth one paragraph. It is not a measurement, and the thesis's paired number does not sharpen it (objection 1b).

On priority of the design: "fork two children from one checkpoint and vary only the post-fork randomness" is the instability protocol of Frankle, Dziugaite, Roy and Carbin (ICML 2020); "each source of nondeterminism alone produces the same end-to-end variance as all of them together" is the headline of Summers and Dinneen (ICML 2021); per-source variance decomposition for benchmarks is Bouthillier et al. (MLSys 2021); "test-set variance across runs is mostly independent example-level flips" is Jordan (ICLR 2024, arXiv 2304.01910) (all REPORTED: from memory of the papers and from search results, not read for this document). The project's pre-decay saturation result reproduces Summers–Dinneen inside adversarial training; its "sixty flips that net to a handful" observation (VERIFIED, `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/METHOD_DIRECTIONS.md` §1.2) is Jordan's picture. None of this is cited anywhere in `docs/`.

**Grade.** NOT ANSWERABLE IN 14 WEEKS as a priority claim; it is not true. Pre-empt in the writing: (i) never write "first" or "the field does not measure"; write "nine of ten ARD method papers report a single run; AT papers since 2020 typically report three to ten seeds", with the table; (ii) cite Frankle 2020, Summers–Dinneen 2021, Bouthillier 2021 and Jordan 2024 as the origin of the design and place the project's contribution as "the first application of a within-parent replicate floor to adversarial training screens, and the measurement that it collapses twelve-fold at the learning-rate decay"; (iii) drop the "0.40 assumed, 0.092 measured" framing — the 0.40 was the project's own working guess (VERIFIED, `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/EVIDENCE_RECLASSIFICATION.md` §0.3: "midpoint of a bracket, not a measurement"), and correcting one's own guess is housekeeping.

## 4. "The 3.04 pp spread across RSLAD re-implementations is a schedule difference you can read off the papers. You call it noise."

**The objection.** The three values are RSLAD's own 51.49 (300 epochs, decay at 215/260/285, best checkpoint), PeerAiD's 51.03 (300 epochs) and AdaAD's 48.45 (200 epochs, decay 100/150, best-PGD-10 checkpoint), the last copied digit-for-digit by DGAD (VERIFIED as recorded with the papers' setup sections in `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/ARD_VERSUS_AT_ASSESSMENT.md` §1.1). The two 300-epoch numbers differ by 0.46 pp; the 200-epoch number differs from them by about 3 pp. RSLAD's Appendix Table 11 shows baselines moving by about 1 pp between schedules (REPORTED via the same table). IGDM's 200-epoch RSLAD with an LTD teacher is 52.13 and this project's 200-epoch RSLAD with the LTD WRN-34-10 teacher is 51.90 (VERIFIED, same document §1.1 and §2.2), so the teacher moves RSLAD by roughly 3.5 pp at fixed schedule as well. None of this is noise; it is schedule and teacher, both stated in the papers.

The thesis document says "None of these papers reports the variation that would let a reader tell a re-implementation difference from a method difference" (VERIFIED, `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/essays/PRIOR_ART_STATE_CONDITIONED_AUGMENTATION.md` §3). The reader does not need reported variation to tell 300 epochs from 200. The honest criticism is protocol heterogeneity and copied baseline rows — real, and different. Calling it noise hands the examiner an easy correction and taints the measurement chapter by association.

**Grade.** ANSWERABLE TODAY, in the writing: attribute the spread to schedule and teacher with the numbers above; reserve "noise" for within-protocol independent-seed SDs.

## 5. "After four months your one reproduced effect is IDBH applied late inside someone else's method, you have not shown the switch beats IDBH throughout, you have not shown it needs a teacher, and its headline number describes a quantity that does not exist."

**The objection.** Five parts.

(a) **I100 is the upstream IDBH weak pipeline, started at epoch 100.** VERIFIED, `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/essays/PRIOR_ART_STATE_CONDITIONED_AUGMENTATION.md` §4 (code and paper). IDBH's own Table 1 shows that stronger augmentation buys its end-of-training advantage mainly by shrinking the best-to-end fall in teacher-free AT (VERIFIED as quoted there). The project's explanation of I100 — hardness pays only where robust overfitting exists, after the decay — is IDBH's mechanism plus Rice et al.'s timing, and is labelled INFERRED in that document. Nothing in it mentions a teacher.

(b) **Switch versus always-on is the comparison that would make the schedule a contribution, and it is unresolved.** IDBH from epoch 0 (I0) versus CropShift is +1.12 / +0.46 pp at e199; I100 is +1.20 / +1.04 (VERIFIED, `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/METHOD_DIRECTIONS.md` §1.3 and `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/ERT_RSLAD_SINGLE_SWITCH_TIMING.md`). The I100-minus-I0 difference is therefore +0.08 / +0.58 pp on two seeds, judged UNDERPOWERED at a k = 2 MDE of 0.79 pp (VERIFIED, `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/EVIDENCE_RECLASSIFICATION.md` row A3). The timing family was rejected as a direction ("no residual question worth 31 GPU-h"; VERIFIED, `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/METHOD_DIRECTIONS.md` §6). Worse, the project's own low-learning-rate hypothesis predicts I0 ≈ I100 in final robustness ("exposure to IDBH before e100 adds nothing"; VERIFIED, §1.3). If that prediction holds, the switch is not better than IDBH throughout, only cheaper, and the result is IDBH's result observed inside RSLAD. The only thing I0 loses in the records is trajectory AUC, whose floor has never been measured (VERIFIED, row A4 UNRESOLVED).

(c) **Teacher-independence is untested, and the one teacher-specific mechanism was never measured.** I100 has not been run under PGD-AT or TRADES. The teacher is a RobustBench checkpoint trained with standard augmentation (VERIFIED, `.external/robustbench/model_info/cifar10/Linf/Chen2021LTD_WRN34_10.json`; augmentation REPORTED from the LTD paper), and RSLAD's soft label is the teacher's output on the augmented clean view (`kl_target: teacher_clean`; VERIFIED, `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/ERT_RSLAD_RNG_SOURCE_DECOMPOSITION.md` training-attack block). Under IDBH the teacher is labelling colour-jittered and partly erased images it never saw in training. No document measures the teacher's accuracy or agreement on IDBH-augmented views versus CropShift views (VERIFIED absence by search of `docs/` for teacher-on-augmentation figures). INFERRED: if teacher label quality falls under IDBH, delaying IDBH to the low-learning-rate phase would be a distillation-specific finding (the student stops trusting degraded labels late), and that is the only route by which I100 becomes a result about distillation. The thesis has not looked.

(d) **The pooled headline is a mixture of two comparisons.** "+0.864 pp, SD 0.247, t(4) = 7.82" averages two dev-seed contrasts against CropShift-throughout (+1.20 / +1.04) with three confirmation contrasts against CropShift-then-RandomErasing (+0.78 / +0.68 / +0.62); the groups do not overlap (VERIFIED, `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/NUMERIC_CONSISTENCY_AUDIT.md` finding #2 and its verification section). The status summary's executive conclusion and the reclassification's proposed thesis paragraph still carry the pooled figure (VERIFIED, `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/ERT_RESEARCH_STATUS_SUMMARY.md`; `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/EVIDENCE_RECLASSIFICATION.md` Part 3(b)).

(e) **"At the decay" is read as a mechanism from a coincidence.** I125 equals I100 to the second decimal on both seeds in robust accuracy (VERIFIED, `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/ERT_RSLAD_SINGLE_SWITCH_TIMING.md`); `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/METHOD_DIRECTIONS.md` §1.3 turns two identical counts on 5,000 images into "exposure during e100–125 adds nothing". Two seeds at an unmeasured e199 floor establish no such thing.

**Grade.** (b) ANSWERABLE WITH I0 on the three confirmation seeds to e199 with AutoAttack: 3 × 5.1 GPU-h plus 6 evaluations, about 21 GPU-h, cheapest experiment in this document with a thesis sentence riding on it. (c) ANSWERABLE TODAY for the teacher-label question: one inference pass of the teacher over the training set under CropShift and under IDBH_WEAK views, minutes of GPU, no training; ANSWERABLE WITH I100 versus its comparator under PGD-AT and corrected TRADES, five seeds each, about 100 GPU-h plus 40 evaluations, for the teacher-independence question. (d) ANSWERABLE TODAY: replace the pooled figure everywhere with the two named contrasts. (e) ANSWERABLE WITH Direction 2 Stage 1 after an e199 floor exists; until then write "switch epoch between e75 and e125 not resolved".

## 6. "The one effect you know to be real is invisible at the horizon where your instrument is sharp. What is the instrument for?"

**The objection.** The floor is measured at e104/e109/e114. The I100 gain over its comparator is +0.18 / −0.04 pp (dev seeds) and +0.01 pp (confirm-b) in the e100–114 window, all under the k = 2 MDE of 0.243 pp; about a third of it exists at e149; most arrives after the second decay at e150 (VERIFIED, `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/METHOD_DIRECTIONS.md` §1.3 window-mean table; `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/ERT_RSLAD_UNSEEN_CONFIRMATION_RESULTS.md` lines 51–56: +0.24 pp at e149, +0.69 pp at e199). The repository says so: "the instrument is sharpest at e114, and the one effect the project knows to be real is absent there" (VERIFIED, §1.3). No paired floor exists at e199 (VERIFIED, §2: "The e199 paired fork floor is not measured"), and whether the floor regrows across the second decay is unknown (VERIFIED, §5 item 1).

INFERRED consequence: every e114 verdict in the project is a verdict about early post-decay behaviour, and the project's best example shows early post-decay behaviour does not predict the final result. A screen that stops at e114 cannot be the gate the measurement standard says it is unless e114 differences are shown to predict e199 differences. The project has one data point and it says they do not.

**Grade.** ANSWERABLE WITH plan 0093's twelve control forks taken to e199 and evaluated at e114/129/149/154/174/199 (31 GPU-h, already proposed in `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/METHOD_DIRECTIONS.md` §5), or plan 0092's six replicates extended to e199 (about 15 GPU-h). Until one exists, e114 screens are descriptive and must be written as such.

## 9. "The comparison you propose has partly been made, the fairness axis is unstated, the teacher is a free parameter on one side only, and the expected answer is already in your own table."

**The objection.** Four points.

(a) The premise that no ARD paper compares against a post-2019 teacher-free baseline holds for the papers examined (VERIFIED, `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/ARD_VERSUS_AT_ASSESSMENT.md` §1.1; `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/SMALL_MODEL_AT_2024_2026.md` §4; a fresh search found nothing further, REPORTED). But the boundary is not where the thesis draws it. CAT (2023) sets a jointly trained ResNet-18 against RSLAD and RSLAD + AWP (VERIFIED as recorded in `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/SMALL_MODEL_AT_2024_2026.md` §4). ADR (ICLR 2024) is AT with self-distillation from an EMA copy at 51.18 AA with five-rerun SDs; LTD, the project's own teacher recipe, is itself distillation (VERIFIED as recorded in `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/ARD_VERSUS_AT_ASSESSMENT.md` §3.2 and §1.2). The thesis must define the comparison as "frozen external robust teacher versus none" and say why that line and not another.

(b) "Fair" on which axis? The design in `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/ARD_VERSUS_AT_ASSESSMENT.md` §4 fixes data. It does not fix compute: the ARD arm consumes a WRN-34-10 teacher whose training cost is several times the student's, plus a teacher forward pass per step (INFERRED). Under a compute-matched rule a teacher-free RN-18 with more epochs or an ensemble is the fair comparator, and the answer may flip. The thesis must state the axis and defend it before the runs, or the examiner picks the other one afterwards.

(c) The teacher is a free parameter on the ARD side only. The project's own §3.3 says the teacher moves RSLAD by 3.5 to 4 pp, more than any method effect (VERIFIED). So "ARD beats AT by X" is really "ARD with this teacher beats AT by X", and the choice of the LTD teacher (strong, no extra data, same family) must be preregistered as the rule, with a weaker and a stronger teacher as sensitivity arms if the budget allows.

(d) The assembled table already gives the expected answer: under fixed data ARD leads by about 0.5 to 1.5 pp on unmatched numbers, and with the teacher's data given directly to the small model, AT leads by 6 to 8 pp (VERIFIED, §1.3 Rules A and B). The teacher-free band now has a seven-run frontier at 52.76 ± 0.14 (VERIFIED as recorded in `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/SMALL_MODEL_AT_2024_2026.md`). The project's RSLAD + I100 at 53.0 to 53.2 (45k train) sits 0.3 to 0.9 pp above it. With independent-seed SDs of 0.14 to 0.34 pp on each side and five seeds per arm, the MDE of the between-arm difference is about 0.5 pp (INFERRED, `2.8 × 0.3 × √(2/5)`). The matched comparison may land exactly at its own resolution limit, and either outcome — "a teacher buys about half a point" or "a teacher buys nothing AWP and augmentation do not" — must be written down in advance as a result.

**Grade.** (a), (b), (c) ANSWERABLE TODAY in the writing. (d) ANSWERABLE WITH the comparison itself (three teacher-free arms × five seeds, about 75 GPU-h, plus AWP work) provided the rule and the axis are preregistered; NOT ANSWERABLE IN 14 WEEKS if AWP slips, in which case the chapter must be titled "against PGD-AT and TRADES under a shared protocol" and name the missing arm.

## 10. "Your proposed method is DGAD's partition applied to IDBH's augmentation; the teacher half of the state passes nine images in ten; the cohort it acts on is three per cent of the data; the sign is undetermined; and every loss-based intervention on the same states failed to transfer."

**The objection.** Six parts, all from the project's own documents.

(a) **Novelty is a recombination and the repository says so.** The student-only gate is a special case of AROID (IJCV 2024). The teacher-relation gate has TST and TeachAugment as ancestors in standard distillation and IAD, DGAD and SAAD as teacher-gated loss analogues in ARD; the verdict is "a recombination, not a new principle" (VERIFIED, `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/essays/PRIOR_ART_STATE_CONDITIONED_AUGMENTATION.md` "Verdict"). The gap is real and narrow.

(b) **The teacher condition carries almost no information on the training set.** The frozen teacher threshold is the 10th percentile of the teacher's margin on the training data (`e100 Teacher q10 = 0.174 / 0.178`; VERIFIED, `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/ERT_RSLAD_I100_ONLINE_STATE_S2_PRESERVATION.md` "Frozen lineage" table). If T1 means "teacher margin above that threshold" (INFERRED from the column name), the teacher condition admits about 90 % of training images. The teacher was trained on these very images (RobustBench checkpoint trained on the full CIFAR-10 training set; REPORTED) and is therefore confident on nearly all of them. INFERRED: on the training set, "student fragile AND teacher confident" is nearly "student fragile", so Variant 2 reduces to Variant 1 — the AROID special case — unless the thesis shows that the 10 % excluded by T1 behave differently. Nothing in the records shows that.

(c) **Cohort size bounds the effect.** S2×T1 is 3.3 % of training samples during e100–114 (VERIFIED, same report, "Online-state mechanics" table: 3.29–3.36 %). Changing the augmentation on 3 % of images cannot plausibly move the whole-split number by more than a small fraction of what a whole-dataset augmentation change produces, and that is 0.7 to 1.1 pp (VERIFIED, I100 contrasts). The prior-art document expects 0.1 to 0.5 pp (VERIFIED, §"Verdict"); INFERRED, 0.05 to 0.3 pp is the realistic range for a 3 % cohort.

(d) **The sign is contested by the literature the project cites.** EntAugment, MADAug and the 2025 influence paper give stronger augmentation to easy samples; IDBH says hardness beyond capacity costs accuracy; the project's own reading concludes the natural gate is the opposite of the proposal (VERIFIED, `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/essays/PRIOR_ART_STATE_CONDITIONED_AUGMENTATION.md` §1 point 3 and §4). A method whose sign is unknown before the first run is a hypothesis.

(e) **The loss lever on the same partitions produced nothing that reached held-out data.** Row E8: per-sample interventions produce train-cohort effects 13 to 33 times the train noise and no resolvable held-out effect, across four campaigns (VERIFIED, `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/EVIDENCE_RECLASSIFICATION.md` E8, STANDS). The examiner will ask why augmentation escapes a pattern every loss-based intervention fell into. The only offered mechanism (transfer to same-state held-out images, cancelled elsewhere) rests on a post-hoc split of two seeds (VERIFIED, `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/METHOD_DIRECTIONS.md` §1.4).

(f) **The timeline does not admit a confirmed result.** The effect, if any, appears after e150 (objection 6). A powered test is six parents × two replicates × four arms (control, student gate, teacher gate, random gate of matched fraction) to e199: about 123 GPU-h plus parents, then an official-test AutoAttack confirmation on fresh seeds (INFERRED sizing from `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/METHOD_DIRECTIONS.md` §2 rates). One cycle is three to four weeks of calendar under the current launch discipline; there is time for one cycle, perhaps two, and none for a negative first cycle followed by a redesign, alongside objections 7 and 8 and the writing.

**Grade.** (a)–(d) ANSWERABLE TODAY in the writing, by presenting the work as a preregistered ablation — "does the teacher relation carry information the student state does not, for the augmentation lever?" — with both directions and a random gate as arms, and by first computing, at zero GPU cost, how many training images the teacher condition actually excludes and what the student-only and teacher-relation cohorts share. (e) ANSWERABLE WITH the 2.9 GPU-h placebo (plan 0095) before anything is built; if a random cohort reproduces the treated effect, the family closes. (f) NOT ANSWERABLE IN 14 WEEKS as a confirmed positive method result. Pre-empt by writing the method chapter as a designed, powered, either-outcome experiment and by not calling it a contribution until it has an official-test confirmation.

## Where the thesis is in better shape than the student may fear

Said once, with evidence, because the instruction asks for it where the evidence supports it.

- The pre-decay floor is a strong, replicated measurement: 84 untreated pairwise comparisons from two campaign families, agreeing at 1.14–1.25 pp, flat in horizon, reproduced by three placebo-based families (VERIFIED, `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/MEASUREMENT_DESIGN.md` §2.4–2.4b), and the twelve-fold collapse of per-run SD across the learning-rate decay (1.088 pp at e99 to 0.226 pp at e104 independent-seed; 0.092 pp paired at e114) is a clean, quantitative, useful statement about when adversarial-training screens can be read. That, not the 0.092 number, is the measurement contribution.
- Bit-determinism across runs, hosts and environments (VERIFIED, `docs/archive/ard-distillation-2026/ERT_RSLAD_REAL_DATA_TRAINING_DETERMINISM.md`; commit 70750bb) is rare in this field and makes every attribution checkable.
- The I100 contrast against the stronger comparator is preregistered, three-seed, official-test, AutoAttack, best and last both reported, clean cost stated, and the best-checkpoint difference is +0.39 ± 0.08 pp (VERIFIED, plan 0091). Few ARD papers meet that standard for their main claim, and the thesis is currently underselling it.
- The self-correction record — TRADES root cause, numeric audit, reclassification, the same-day discovery that the new floor was of the wrong kind for the old screens — is a methodological asset if presented as method.

None of this rescues a method contribution. It supports a thesis whose contribution is a measurement standard for adversarial-training screens, one honestly reported augmentation-schedule result, and one matched comparison if the AT side is built in time.
