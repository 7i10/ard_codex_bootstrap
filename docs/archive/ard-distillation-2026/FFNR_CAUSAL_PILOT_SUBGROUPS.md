# FFNR causal pilot subgroup analysis

This is a CPU-only reanalysis of the completed Chen causal pilot (L2 seed 1,
L4 seed 2, endpoint epoch 83). It does not select a new treatment or use the
official test set.

## Matched selected versus random effects

The per-sample effect is `robust_correct(endpoint) -
robust_correct(C79 parent)`. Rescue is parent-wrong to endpoint-correct;
harm is parent-correct to endpoint-wrong. Random masks are count-matched and
class-matched, but they are not required to be disjoint from selected masks;
the JSON report records their overlap.

| seed | route | arm | n | net effect | rescue | harm |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| L2 | Route A | selected | 5,667 | 1.50% | 4.15% | 2.65% |
| L2 | Route A | random | 5,667 | 1.76% | 4.55% | 2.79% |
| L2 | Route B | selected | 1,124 | 2.49% | 9.88% | 7.38% |
| L2 | Route B | random | 1,124 | 1.33% | 8.72% | 7.38% |
| L4 | Route A | selected | 5,755 | 1.13% | 4.38% | 3.25% |
| L4 | Route A | random | 5,755 | 0.90% | 4.57% | 3.67% |
| L4 | Route B | selected | 1,138 | 3.43% | 10.54% | 7.12% |
| L4 | Route B | random | 1,138 | 2.11% | 9.14% | 7.03% |

Class-stratified sample bootstrap uses 2,000 replicates and seed `20260809`.
The selected-minus-random net-effect differences are:

| seed | route | difference | 95% CI |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| L2 | Route A | −0.26 pp | [−1.27, +0.71] pp |
| L2 | Route B | +1.16 pp | [−2.31, +4.50] pp |
| L4 | Route A | +0.23 pp | [−0.80, +1.27] pp |
| L4 | Route B | +1.32 pp | [−2.09, +4.64] pp |

The point estimates are compatible with the earlier observation that Route B
is directionally better than its matched random control, but all intervals
include zero. This is a pilot signal, not evidence for a confirmed treatment
effect.

## Teacher/student subgroup decomposition

The JSON report partitions each selected/random arm by teacher clean/adversarial
correctness, student clean correctness, quartile of the parent robust-margin
EMA, and quartile of the teacher clean-to-adversarial margin response. It also
records class-wise treatment effects and robust-margin EMA changes. This
directly separates:

- teacher clean-correct / adversarial-wrong samples, where adversarial KD may be
  unsafe;
- teacher-correct samples where KD should be preserved;
- student-clean-wrong samples, where the issue is not robustness alone; and
- low-margin boundary samples.

The human panel has only role-blind panel row keys, not the full stable train
sample-ID mapping, so it is not silently joined here. The panel remains
available for a future join once its ID map is recovered.

Raw CPU output (ignored) is `.cache/ffnr-causal-pilot-subgroups-v1.json` and is
generated by `scripts/analyze_causal_pilot_subgroups.py`.
